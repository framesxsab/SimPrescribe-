# SimpliScribe Product Requirements

## Product promise

Convert difficult-to-read prescriptions into structured medicine requirements and connect patients with approved local pharmacies for understanding, availability, pharmacist verification, and ordering.

The platform helps patients understand and fulfil an existing prescription; it does not perform medical diagnosis.

## Users and outcomes

- Patients register, upload, review, and retain short-lived prescription history; find pharmacies by PIN; request and accept COD pickup/delivery quotes; and track orders.
- Pharmacies register for approval, maintain exact-name inventory, verify the original prescription, quote every line, and update fulfillment.
- Administrators approve pharmacies. Reviewers retain the existing professional prescription-review workflow.

## Safety boundaries

- OCR and patient edits never become clinical authority. Pharmacists compare all requested lines with the source document.
- Generic/equivalent entries are clearly labeled information and cannot be ordered as automatic substitutes.
- No diagnosis, prescribing, online payments, split orders, GPS claims, reminders, interactions, logistics, email, or SMS.
